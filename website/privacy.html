Waitlist privacy

The waitlist records the email you submit, the signup time, and a source label such as “website”. These details are used to contact you about Guh Bot hosted availability. No payment or account is created.

In the local development server, entries are stored on the host computer. In the AWS deployment, entries are stored in the operator's DynamoDB table. They are not published to GitHub.

To request removal or ask about your information, contact the project maintainer using the contact options on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not post your email in a public issue. No analytics or advertising cookies are used by this page.
