A few things,
before you start.

What do I need to get started?

A computer, Node.js and one model connection. Use an installed agent CLI or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint. The self-hosting guide walks you through it.

Can I use my own model?

Yes. Connect an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, including a local model server, or use your installed Codex, Claude Code, or OpenCode CLI. Your provider's fees and limits apply.

What can my phone do?

Pair with your host to follow progress and read results. Your desktop can grant operator access so you can send tasks and approve actions. The host must be running and reachable through a secure connection or private network.

Is managed hosting available?

Managed hosting is in development. The waitlist lets us know you're interested. It creates no subscription, and there is no payment. Self-hosting is available in the open-source repository.
